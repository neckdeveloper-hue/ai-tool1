Security

Financial data deserves financial-grade security

Security in FCEN.AI is implemented, not promised — these are the mechanisms running in the product today.

OTP + two-factor authentication

Password sign-in supports TOTP 2FA and backup codes; mobile sign-in uses rate-limited, hashed OTPs — no social logins.

Hashed credentials

Passwords use salted scrypt hashing with per-user salts. Session cookies are HttpOnly and last 7 days.

Audited administration

Every admin action — pricing, country settings, activations, rate data — is written to an immutable audit log.

Verified backups

Scheduled local backups with integrity checks and retention, plus optional encrypted off-site storage.

Firm-scoped isolation

Every record — clients, ledgers, documents, findings — is filtered by firm at the query layer. Cross-firm access returns not-found, not details.

Honest by design

No feature pretends to be finished. Roadmap items say roadmap; OCR says when the engine is not installed; tax quotes cite their source.

FAQ

Questions, answered honestly

Which countries does FCEN.AI support?

India and the United States. Sign-up uses +91 or +1 mobile numbers (or email + password), pricing is in INR or USD, and each edition carries its country's accounting and tax modules. Switzerland is not a supported market.

What is included in the India edition?

GST tools (GSTR-1/3B/2B, ITC reconciliation), TDS, Income Tax, depreciation, loan calculations, Clause 44 reporting, exception center, compliance radar and the full client-operations workspace. Tally integration is on the roadmap and labelled as such.

What is included in the USA edition?

A complete double-entry US accounting system (chart of accounts, journal, trial balance, P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, AR/AP aging), a jurisdiction-aware sales tax engine with exemptions and nexus tracking, invoice/bill management with automatic posting, bank reconciliation from OFX/QBO/CSV statements, and an OCR document inbox. Federal tax modules are architecture-stage — never presented as filing-ready.

How does the sales tax engine stay accurate?

Rates are effective-dated rows targeting state, county, city or ZIP, maintained and verified by the firm owner against official state revenue department datasets. Every quote cites its source and flags unverified or state-only estimates. FCEN.AI is not a filing service.

Is my data used to train AI models?

No. There is no AI API integrated in the product today. The planned tax-advisory assistant is architecture-stage and will require explicit owner approval, retrieval from authoritative sources and human CA/CPA review before it ever gives consequential advice.

How do I get started?

Create a firm with email and password, or sign in with a mobile OTP (+91 / +1). Every plan includes a 7-day free trial — no card required to start, and you can upgrade, downgrade or cancel from the billing panel.
